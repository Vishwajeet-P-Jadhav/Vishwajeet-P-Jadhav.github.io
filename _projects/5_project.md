## Overview

I spent my summer of 2025 at the University of Southern California, Los Angeles as a part of the [IUSST-Viterbi program](https://iusstf.org/iusstf-viterbi-program) where I undertook research activities at the [ACME lab](https://acme.usc.edu/) led by Prof. Constantine Sideris. My projects were focussing on biomedical circuits.

## Project Details

**Duration:** May 2025 – July 2025  
**Role:** Summer Research Intern 
**Advisor:** [Prof. Constantine Sideris](https://minghsiehece.usc.edu/directory/faculty/profile/?lname=Sideris&fname=Constantine), Associate Professor, Electrical and Computer Engineering, USC

## Research Objectives

1. Designing PCBs for testing a custom neurostimulator chip
2. Assisting with the IC measurements and characterization
3. Contributing to he design of a new PLL-based IC for bio-assay in TSMC65nm technology node

## Technologies used
    - Cadence virtuoso
    - MATLAB
    - KiCAD
